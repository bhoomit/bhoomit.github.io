Tag guide

How to read the idea, wording, source, and editorial tags.

I · Idea
Who contributed the idea expressed in this span.

W · Wording
Who supplied the wording. Human wording stays human when AI only cleans or rephrases it; substantive new writing is assessed separately.

Ed · Editorial
Edited by AI: cleanup or rephrasing of supplied wording. This is a separate contribution, not shared wording authorship.

Source · N / F / R
Novel, other fiction, or research. A borrowed premise differs from an original application of it.

← Introduced by
Who brought that source into this piece. This credits source selection, not authorship of the source.

H Human · A AI · H+A Both · E External · ? Unresolved. A trailing ~ means provisional. Shared requires substantive contributions, not just approval.

Example: a novel-derived premise
I: ExternalW: AISource: NovelIntroduced by Human
The novel supplies the premise; the human brings it into the argument; AI composes this wording. An original analogy using that novel can still have a human idea tag.
